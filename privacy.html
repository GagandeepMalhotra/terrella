Privacy

Last updated 30 September 2026

The short version

Terrella collects nothing about you. It has no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and it sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

What the app and the mod do

  • The Minecraft mod runs on your own PC. It sends the world you are playing to the headset over your home network, and nowhere else.
  • The headset app finds your PC on the same Wi‑Fi and receives that world. It does not connect to the internet.
  • Your settings (size, height, display options) are saved on the headset only.
  • To place the world in your room, the app uses the room layout and anchors your headset already provides. These stay on the headset; the app never reads or sends camera images.

Your home network

While Minecraft is running with the mod, any device on the same network can connect to it and see the world you are playing. Use it on a network you trust.

Deleting your data

There is no data held anywhere but your own devices. Uninstalling the app removes its settings; removing the mod file from your mods folder removes the mod.
