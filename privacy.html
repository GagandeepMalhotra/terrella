Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is handled in connection with Terrella, which consists of the Terrella application for Meta Quest headsets (the “App”), the Terrella modification for Minecraft: Java Edition (the “Mod”) and this website (together, the “Services”).

The Services are provided by Gagandeep Malhotra, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom (“we”, “us”). For the purposes of the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018, we are the controller of any personal data described in this Policy.

2. Personal data we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal data through the App or the Mod. Neither contains analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and neither requires an account.

If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and the contents of your message, and will use them only to respond to you.

3. Data processed on your own devices

To operate, the Services process the following information entirely on devices you own. None of it is transmitted to us or to any third party.

  • Game data. The Mod reads the Minecraft world you are playing, including blocks, entities, other players’ names and skins, and, if you enable it, chat messages. It sends this data to the App over your local network.
  • Room and spatial data. The App uses the room layout, surfaces and spatial anchors provided by the Meta Quest operating system in order to place the hologram. This data remains on the headset. The App does not access camera images.
  • Hand and controller input. The App uses hand-tracking and controller input, as provided by the operating system, solely to respond to your interactions in real time. This data is not stored.
  • Settings. Your preferences are stored locally on the headset.

4. Local network communication

While Minecraft is running with the Mod installed, the Mod accepts connections from devices on the same local network so that the App can locate and connect to it. Any device on that network could connect to the Mod and receive the game data described in section 3. You should use the Services only on a network you trust.

5. Third parties

  • Meta Platforms. Your use of a Meta Quest headset is governed by Meta’s own terms and privacy policy. We receive no data from Meta about you.
  • GitHub. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical information, such as IP addresses, when you visit it. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.
  • Buy Me a Coffee. Voluntary support is handled by Buy Me a Coffee. Any information you provide there is processed under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your payment details.

6. Data retention

Because we do not collect personal data through the App or the Mod, we retain none. Email correspondence is retained only for as long as necessary to deal with your enquiry.

7. Children

The Services do not collect personal data from any user, including children.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability in respect of your personal data. As the App and Mod hold no personal data, these rights will normally apply only to email correspondence. You may remove all data held by the App and the Mod by uninstalling the App and deleting the Mod file from your Minecraft mods folder. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The effective date above will be revised whenever changes are made.

10. Contact

For any question about this Policy or to exercise your rights, contact gagandeepmalhotra1234@gmail.com.
