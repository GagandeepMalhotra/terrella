Terrella

The world you're playing, in the room you're in.

Get started What you need

What you need

  • Meta Quest 3 or 3S

    With the Terrella app.

  • Minecraft Java Edition

    1.19.4 to 26.2, with Fabric.

  • One Wi‑Fi network

    Your PC and headset on the same one.

What you see

  • Straight from your game

    Blocks, mobs and items come from Minecraft itself, so mods and resource packs just work.

  • As it happens

    Weather, day and night, light and particles, in step with the game.

  • Table or floor

    Set it on a table, or lay the world across your floor.

  • Hands or controllers

    Place, move and resize it either way.

Questions

Does the server need the mod?

No. It runs only in your game, so it works on any server and in single player.

Do shader packs show up?

No. Resource packs do; shader packs change only the picture on your screen, which the headset never sees.

The headset can't find Minecraft.

Check both are on the same Wi‑Fi, then use Enter address with the address Minecraft shows in chat when you join a world.
