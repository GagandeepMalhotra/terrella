Terrella

See your Minecraft world as a live 3D hologram in your room.

Get started

What you need

  • Meta Quest 3 or 3S

    Mixed reality, with the Terrella app.

  • Minecraft Java Edition

    1.19.4 to 26.2 with Fabric, or 1.20.1 to 26.2 with NeoForge.

  • Wi‑Fi network

    Your PC and headset on the same one.

Features

  • Live while you play

    Build, dig and explore in Minecraft, and see it change on your desk.

  • Works with your mods and resource packs

  • Place on any surface

  • Custom world size

    Pick how big it is and how much of the world it shows.

  • Any dimension

  • Works in multiplayer

    On servers too, with every player and their name.

  • Globe view

    Curve the world into a planet that turns as you walk.

See it on Meta Quest 3

FAQs

Does the server need the mod?

No. It runs only in your game, so it works on any server and in single player.

Do shader packs show up?

No. Resource packs do; shader packs change only the picture on your screen, which the headset never sees.
