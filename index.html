Terrella

See your Minecraft world as a live 3D hologram in your room.

Get started

What you need

  • Meta Quest 3 or 3S

    Mixed reality, with the Terrella app.

  • Minecraft Java Edition

    1.19.4 to 26.2, with Fabric.

  • Wi‑Fi network

    Your PC and headset on the same one.

Features

  • Live from your game

    Blocks, mobs, items, water, weather, day and night, updated as you play.

  • Your mods and resource packs

    They show up too, because every picture comes from Minecraft itself.

  • On a table or your floor

    Place the world on a table, or lay it across your floor.

  • Any size and height

    Resize it, and choose how much above and below you to show.

  • Hands or controllers

    Pinch or pull the trigger to place, move and resize it.

  • See yourself through blocks

    When you go underground, a faint outline shows exactly where you are in the world.

  • Free and private

    No account, no ads, and nothing collected.

FAQs

Does the server need the mod?

No. It runs only in your game, so it works on any server and in single player.

Do shader packs show up?

No. Resource packs do; shader packs change only the picture on your screen, which the headset never sees.
